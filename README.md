## 👋 About me

I build AI products end to end, from the first architecture sketch to the thing real people use every day. My focus is agentic AI and LLM systems: agents that call tools, MCP servers that plug models into real business workflows, and the SaaS platforms wrapped around them. As CTO at Citadel Technologies BV in Stockholm, I care just as much about security, compliance and reliability as I do about shipping fast. I also love building teams, and through AI Horizon Global / IAII I am hiring AI engineers and researchers who want to do the same.

## 🎯 What I focus on

- 🤖 **Agentic AI and LLM apps**: tool-using agents, RAG, prompt and model orchestration across hosted and local models
- 🔌 **MCP servers**: exposing real business workflows to Claude, Cursor and other MCP clients
- ☁️ **AI SaaS**: full-stack products on TypeScript, React / Next.js, Node and Supabase
- 👁️ **Computer vision** and data-driven analytics
- 🩺 **Health tech**: wellness tracking, lab report analysis and care pathways
- 🛒 **B2B marketplaces**: RFQs, matching, offers and orders
- 🛡️ **Security and compliance**: ISO 27001 ISMS tooling, log analysis, endpoint telemetry
- 🦀 **Rust** for fast, reliable system agents
